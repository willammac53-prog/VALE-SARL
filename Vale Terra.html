Privacy Policy

VALE Terra · Published by VALE SARL (Perper lux Store) · Effective date: November 24, 2026

In short: VALE Terra works entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the VALE Terra mobile application (the “App”), published by VALE SARL (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The App has no user accounts, no registration, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your name, email address, location, device identifiers, usage statistics or any content you create.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you keep your work between sessions, the App stores the data you enter (for example projects, borehole logs, calculations, soil and land assessments, irrigation calculations and notes) in the App’s private storage on your device. This data:

  • never leaves your device unless you choose to copy it yourself;
  • is not accessible to us or to any third party;
  • is removed when you use Settings → Clear all data or when you uninstall the App.

4. Permissions

The App does not request any Android permissions. It does not use the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

5. Clipboard

When you tap a “Copy report” button, the App places a plain-text report on your device clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. This only happens when you request it. The App does not read the clipboard.

6. Third parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services, and it does not connect to any server. We therefore do not share data with any third party.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a professional field tool and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the App’s private storage area, protected by the Android operating system. Because the App is offline, there is no transmission of data that could be intercepted. Please keep your device secure and back up important information yourself, as we cannot recover data that is deleted or lost with the device.

9. Accuracy of results

The calculations in the App are preliminary estimates. They do not replace professional engineering judgement, site investigation or manufacturer data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at the same web address, with the effective date shown above.
