Our services

Built on geology — not guesswork

From the first field survey to the last water test, VALE SARL covers the full life cycle of a water source and the land around it.

Water Well Drilling

Rotary and percussion drilling for domestic, agricultural and commercial supply — full casing, gravel pack and well development included.

Pumps & Water Systems

Submersible, surface and solar pumping, pressure sets, tanks and filtration — supplied, commissioned and maintained.

Agricultural Land Evaluation

Soil profiling, drainage and slope assessment, texture and structure analysis, and land capability classification for purchase, lease or crop planning.

Hydrogeological Studies

Desk study, field survey and groundwater mapping to identify the most productive and sustainable drilling points before a rig moves in.

Irrigation Design & Installation

Drip and sprinkler systems sized to your plot, water source and crop — with smart controllers, filtration and winterisation.

Water Quality & Compliance

Sampling and laboratory analysis for potability, nitrates, iron and hardness, plus support with the French declaration paperwork.

Privacy Policy

1. What data we collect

  • Business data: correspondence, quotes, surveys and reports produced during a project.

2. Why we process your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.

  • To improve the performance and security of our website.

3. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent (contact form), the performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps, our legitimate interest in operating and securing our website, and our legal obligations.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiry data: 3 years from the last contact.
  • Contractual and accounting records: 10 years, as required by French law.
  • Technical and analytics data: up to 13 months.

5. Who we share it with

Your data is never sold. It may be shared with our hosting provider, analytics provider, accountants and — where strictly necessary for a project — subcontractors and laboratories, all bound by confidentiality obligations. Some providers may process data outside the EU; in that case transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies required for it to function, plus optional analytics cookies. You can refuse or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; essential cookies cannot be disabled without affecting site functionality.

7. Your rights

  • Data portability.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés — cnil.fr).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@sarlwater.space. We will respond within one month.

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures — encrypted transmission, access control and regular backups — to protect your data against loss, alteration or unauthorised access.
